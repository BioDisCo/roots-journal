# Brunn–Minkowski proof dependency

`Basic.lean`, `PrekopaLeindler.lean`, and `BrunnMinkowski.lean` are adapted from
[hojonathanho/isoperimetric](https://github.com/hojonathanho/isoperimetric),
commit `29768f8beeaf17295cdf3853d37da35d7e2b0a5f`.
The upstream Apache-2.0 license is reproduced in `LICENSE`.

Local changes: imports and declarations are placed under
`AsymptoticSubspace.BrunnMinkowski`; compatibility fixes target Lean/mathlib
4.35.0. These are source proofs rebuilt locally, not assumed inequalities.
The public symmetrization results are covered by the transitive axiom audit.
